# DEMON SLAYER: KIMETSU NO YAIBA

## A. Introduction

*Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba* is a popular Japanese anime series based on the manga written by Koyoharu Gotouge. The story follows a young boy named Tanjiro Kamado, who becomes a demon slayer after his family is attacked by demons. This anime is known for its amazing animation, exciting battles, emotional story and strong family values.

## B. Synopsis

The story is about Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy who lives with his family in the mountains. One day, he returns home and discovers that his family has been killed by demons, while his younger sister Nezuko has turned into a demon. Tanjiro decides to become a Demon Slayer to find a way to turn his sister back into a human. Along his journey, he meets new friends and faces powerful demons who threaten innocent people. Together, they experience difficult battles and learn the importance of courage, friendship, love, and never giving up.

### Main Themes

- Family and love
- Friendship and teamwork
- Courage and determination
- Sacrifice and kindness
- Hope and perseverance

## C. Characters

The following are five important characters in the series:

1. **Tanjiro Kamado** - The main character who is kind, brave, and determined to protect his sister.
2. **Nezuko Kamado** - Tanjiro's younger sister who becomes a demon but still cares deeply for humans.
3. **Zenitsu Agatsuma** - Tanjiro's friend who is often afraid but becomes powerful when fighting.
4. **Inosuke Hashibira** - A strong and energetic demon slayer who wears a boar mask.
5. **Giyu Tomioka** - A skilled Water Hashira who helps Tanjiro and Nezuko at the beginning of their journey.

> "No matter how many people you may lose, you have no choice but to go on living." — Demon Slayer theme
